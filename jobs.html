Community Outreach Coordinator

Reference: REV001

Engage with local communities to promote volunteer opportunities and foster partnerships.

Salary: $45,000 - $55,000 per annum

Reports to: Community Services Manager

Key Responsibilities

  • Coordinate community events and outreach programs
  • Develop and maintain relationships with local stakeholders
  • Monitor and report on program outcomes

Essential Requirements

  1. Excellent communication and interpersonal skills
  2. Experience in community engagement or related field
  3. Valid driver’s license and access to reliable transportation

Preferable Requirements

  • Bilingual proficiency (English and an additional language)
  • Previous volunteer coordination or project management experience
