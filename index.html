Acknowledgement of Country

Respecting Country, culture and community

ClickJacking Community Help acknowledges the Traditional Custodians of the lands and waterways where we volunteer and gather. We pay respect to Elders past and present and recognise the continuing connection of Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples to Country, culture and community.

Our community work is guided by listening, respect, consent and meaningful local relationships.
